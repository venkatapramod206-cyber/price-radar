Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025 · Effective immediately

This Privacy Policy describes how PricePro ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our website at venkatapramod206-cyber.github.io/price-radar.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored locally in your browser)
  • Price alerts: Product names, target prices, and email addresses you enter for price alerts
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Email addresses for our newsletter
  • Contact form: Name, email, and messages sent through our contact form

Information collected automatically

  • Local storage data: Wishlist items, compare lists, recently viewed products, and preferences stored in your browser's local storage
  • Usage data: Pages visited, search queries, and interactions with the site (through analytics, if enabled)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our price comparison services
  • To send price drop notifications when alerts are triggered
  • To send newsletters if you have subscribed
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve the website
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage

Most of your data (wishlist, preferences, account info) is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This means:

  • Your data stays on your device
  • Clearing your browser data will delete this information
  • We do not transmit this data to our servers

Email addresses provided for price alerts and newsletters are stored securely and used only for the stated purpose.

4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for essential functionality such as theme preferences. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Third-party stores linked from our site may use their own cookies when you visit them.

5. Affiliate Links

Our site contains affiliate links to Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, and other stores. When you click these links, those stores may track your visit using cookies. Please refer to each store's privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Fonts: We load fonts from Google's servers, which may log your IP address
  • Anthropic Claude API: If you use the AI chat feature, your messages may be processed by Anthropic's API

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Unsubscribe from newsletters at any time
  • Delete your locally stored data by clearing browser storage

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@pricepro.in

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@pricepro.in or through our Contact page.
