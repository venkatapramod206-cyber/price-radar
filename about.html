Frequently Asked Questions

Is PricePro free to use?
Yes, completely free! We earn a small affiliate commission when you purchase through our links, at no extra cost to you. This is how we keep the lights on.
How often are prices updated?
Prices are updated every hour for most products. Flash sale prices and time-limited deals may update more frequently.
Which stores do you cover?
We cover 50+ stores including Amazon India, Flipkart, Myntra, Croma, Reliance Digital, Vijay Sales, AJIO, Nykaa, Snapdeal, Tata Cliq, Meesho, and many more.
How do price alerts work?
Go to the Price Alerts page, enter the product name, your target price, and email. We'll notify you by email when the price drops to your target on any tracked store.
Are the reviews on product pages real?
The reviews shown are representative samples aggregated from verified purchases across stores. For the most comprehensive reviews, we recommend checking the individual store pages.
How do I report an incorrect price?
Use our Contact page to report any pricing errors. We aim to fix issues within 24 hours. Always verify the final price on the store's website before completing your purchase.

Affiliate Disclosure

PricePro participates in various affiliate marketing programs. This means we may earn a commission when you click on links to products and make a purchase. This comes at no additional cost to you — you pay the same price whether or not you use our links.

Our editorial content is not influenced by affiliate relationships. We always recommend the best deals regardless of commission rates.
