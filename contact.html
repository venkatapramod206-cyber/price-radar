Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel a price alert?
Go to the Price Alerts page and click the 🗑️ delete button next to any alert.
The price on the store is different from PricePro
Prices change rapidly. Always verify the final price on the store's website before purchasing. PricePro updates prices hourly but flash deals can change within minutes.
I can't find a product I'm looking for
Try different keywords — use the brand name, model number, or category. If still not found, use the contact form to suggest it and we'll add it.
How do I delete my account?
Your account data is stored locally in your browser. You can clear it anytime by going to your browser settings → Clear Site Data. Or email us and we'll guide you.
Do the coupon codes actually work?
The codes shown are representative of real promotional offers available at major Indian stores. Always verify at checkout — bank-specific offers may require a linked card.
